Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

The short version: your files are processed entirely inside your own browser. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else, and we can't see them. We don't use accounts, cookies, analytics or ads.

1. Who we are

MysticTools (“we”, “us”) is a set of PDF and image tools operated by MysticFox. You can reach us at contact@mysticfox.dev.

2. Your files stay on your device

When you use a tool, the file is read and processed by code running in your browser tab (using WebAssembly). The result is created on your device and saved by your browser. No part of your files — not their contents, names or sizes — is sent to our servers or to any third party.

This is enforced technically: the site's Content Security Policy only allows the page to load its own code and not to send data to other websites. You can confirm it yourself in your browser's developer tools (Network tab) while using a tool.

3. What we don't collect

  • We don't collect or store your documents, images, passwords you type into tools, or anything you create with them.
  • We don't use accounts or sign-in.
  • We don't use cookies, analytics, advertising, tracking pixels or fingerprinting.

4. What is stored on your device

To make the app convenient, a few things are kept in your browser's local storage on your device only. We can't access them:

  • your light/dark theme choice;
  • your favourite and recently used tools;
  • workflows you save;
  • the app's own files (and, if you use OCR, its language data) so it loads faster and works offline.

You can remove all of this at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

5. Hosting

The website files are delivered by our hosting provider, Cloudflare, Inc.. Like any website, the provider handles technical data needed to deliver and protect the site, such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request, under its own privacy policy. This never includes your files, because they never leave your device.

6. Security

Processing happens in memory in your browser tab. When you close the tab, the working data is gone. Downloaded results are saved wherever your browser saves files.

7. Children

MysticTools is a general-purpose utility and does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, including children.

8. Your rights

Because we don't collect personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have questions or a request under a data-protection law that applies to you (such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 or the GDPR), contact us at contact@mysticfox.dev.

9. Changes

If we change how the app handles data, we'll update this page and the date at the top. We will not start uploading your files without making that clear on the page itself.
